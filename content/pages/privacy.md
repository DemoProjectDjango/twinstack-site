This site is a set of static files. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no advertising scripts.

**What we collect.** If you email us, book a call or send a message through a form, we hold what you send in order to reply. We do not sell it, and we do not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.

**Analytics.** If privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics are enabled, they record page URL, referrer, country and device type in aggregate. No individual profile is built.

**Third parties.** Booking links go to Google Calendar, WhatsApp links go to WhatsApp, and AppExchange links go to Salesforce. Their own privacy terms apply once you follow them.

**Your data.** To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email {{ site.contact.email }} and we will action it within 30 days.
