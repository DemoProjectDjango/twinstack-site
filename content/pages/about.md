## How we work

We are deliberately small. Engagements are run by the people who write the code, so there is no layer between the person you brief and the person who builds it. That constrains how much we take on at once, and it is the main reason clients stay for years rather than projects.

Three habits define most of our work:

- **Architecture before code.** Governor limits, sharing model and data volume decisions are cheap to change in week one and expensive in month six.
- **Everything in version control.** Scratch-org pipelines, pull requests and repeatable deployments, handed over to you at the end.
- **Security Review treated as a design input.** Reviewers ask the same questions every time. Designing for those questions up front is far cheaper than answering findings later.

## The team
