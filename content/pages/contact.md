## What to send

A short description of the problem gets a better answer than a formal brief. Useful details, if you have them:

- What the app or org does today, and who uses it
- Whether you are pre-listing, in Security Review, or already live
- Your rough timeline and what is driving it
- Any constraint you already know about: edition, data volume, compliance, existing integrations

## What happens next

We reply within one business day, usually with two or three questions. If it looks like a fit we run a free 30-minute call, then send a written scope with a fixed price or a retainer shape. Nothing starts until you have that in writing.
