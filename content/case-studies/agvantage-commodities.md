## The situation

AgVantage trades agricultural commodities, where the same counterparty may appear on both sides of a position and a contract's value moves with the market. Their process ran across email threads, spreadsheets and institutional memory. The cost was not a dramatic failure but a constant tax: slow answers, duplicated entry, and reporting that was always slightly out of date.

## What we did

We modelled contracts, counterparties and positions as related records, so a question like "what is our exposure to this counterparty" became a report rather than an investigation. Data entry moved to the point where information arrives, and validation caught the inconsistencies that previously surfaced weeks later.

## The result

Trading activity lives in one place, position reporting runs on current data, and onboarding a new team member no longer depends on someone explaining where the real spreadsheet is kept.
